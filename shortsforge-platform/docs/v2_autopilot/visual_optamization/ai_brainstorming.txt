I'd split assets into 5 categories
Type	Needed?	Can be free forever?	Quality
Animated icons	✅	Yes	Excellent
Motion graphics	✅	Yes	Excellent
Backgrounds	✅	Mostly	Good
Characters	✅	Harder	Medium
Effects (rain, smoke, fire...)	✅	Yes	Excellent

The first, second and fifth categories are basically solved.

One thing I think is missing from your current thinking

You're still assuming the assets need to exist already.

Recent progress suggests another direction:

There are now research models that generate Lottie/vector animations directly from prompts instead of raster videos. These are early-stage research rather than production-ready tools, but they point toward a future where your pipeline could generate editable JSON animations instead of expensive videos.

That fits your architecture much better than video generation.

If I were building this as a startup

I would spend the next month creating Visual Engine v1, not a video generator.

Its job would be:

🎬 Render with Remotion
🎭 Play Lottie animations
🎮 Play Rive characters
🧩 Animate SVGs procedurally
🌧️ Add particles (rain, smoke, fog, sparks)
📹 Apply camera moves
🎵 Add sound effects
📝 Render captions
🤖 Take a single timeline.json as input

I actually think that renderer is the real product. Once it exists, you can swap in different story generators, TTS engines, or future AI models without redesigning the video pipeline.
